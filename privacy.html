Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

TikTok Creator Analytics is a content analytics tool designed to help users analyze and improve TikTok content performance.

2. Information We Access

When a TikTok account owner authorizes the application through TikTok Login Kit, the service may access information permitted by the authorized TikTok API scopes.

This may include basic TikTok account information and public video information, such as video details and publicly available engagement metrics.

3. How We Use Information

The information accessed through TikTok is used to analyze TikTok video performance, identify content performance trends, support future content creation, and improve social media management.

4. Data Sharing

Information obtained through the TikTok API is not sold to third parties and is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Authorization and Access

TikTok information is accessed only after the TikTok account owner explicitly authorizes the application through TikTok Login Kit. Access may be revoked through TikTok account settings.

6. Data Retention

Information obtained through the TikTok API is retained only as necessary for the operation and analysis purposes of this service.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information accessed through the service from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
